📱 UNIT-CHAPTER-TOPIC PROCESSOR - SIMPLE GUIDE

Hey! 👋 Here's how to use this tool

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎯 WHAT IT DOES:

TWO OPTIONS to process your data!

OPTION 1️⃣: Unit → All Chapters (comma-separated)
OPTION 2️⃣: Separate CSV for each Chapter with topics

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📋 YOUR CSV MUST HAVE:

3 columns exactly:
✅ Unit (ex: "Indian Polity (UNIT 4)")
✅ Chapter (ex: "Evolution of Indian Constitution")
✅ Topic (ex: "Regulating act of 1773")

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ STEP 1: UPLOAD FILE

• Click "Upload your CSV file"
• Select your file
• See preview ✅

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎯 STEP 2: CHOOSE OPTION

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📊 OPTION 1: UNIT-CHAPTER MAPPING

What it does:
Combines all chapters under one unit in single row

Example Input:
Unit: Indian Polity (UNIT 4)
Chapters: Evolution of Constitution
         Making of Constitution
         Preamble

Example Output:
Unit                    | Chapters
Indian Polity (UNIT 4)  | Evolution of Constitution, Making of Constitution, Preamble

How to use:
1️⃣ Go to "Option 1" tab
2️⃣ Click "🚀 Generate Unit-Chapter Mapping"
3️⃣ See result table
4️⃣ Download as CSV or Excel ✅

Perfect for:
✅ Quick reference
✅ Course outlines
✅ Study guides

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📁 OPTION 2: TOPIC SEGREGATION

What it does:
Creates separate CSV for EACH chapter with all its topics

Example Input:
Same as above

Example Output (ZIP file):
📦 Topics_by_Chapter.zip
  📄 Evolution_of_Indian_Constitution.csv
  📄 Making_of_Indian_Constitution.csv
  📄 Preamble.csv
  📄 SUMMARY.txt

How to use:
1️⃣ Go to "Option 2" tab
2️⃣ Check ☑️ "Include summary" (optional)
3️⃣ Click "🚀 Generate ZIP File"
4️⃣ Download ZIP
5️⃣ Extract to see all chapter files ✅

Perfect for:
✅ Separate study materials
✅ Distribute by chapter
✅ Organized learning

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💡 REAL EXAMPLE:

Your CSV (34 rows):
Unit: Indian Polity (UNIT 4)
  Chapter: Evolution of Constitution (11 topics)
  Chapter: Making of Constitution (6 topics)
  Chapter: Preamble (4 topics)

Unit: Indian Economy (UNIT 5)
  Chapter: Basics of Economy (8 topics)
  ...

OPTION 1 gives you:
1 file with 2 rows:
Row 1: Indian Polity | Evolution, Making, Preamble
Row 2: Indian Economy | Basics, Nature, Five-year

OPTION 2 gives you:
ZIP with 6 files:
- Evolution_of_Constitution.csv
- Making_of_Constitution.csv
- Preamble.csv
- Basics_of_Economy.csv
- Nature_of_Indian_Economy.csv
- Five_year_plan.csv

❓ QUICK FAQ:

Q: Can I use both options?
A: YES! Process same file both ways

Q: Which option should I use?
A: Option 1 → Quick overview
   Option 2 → Detailed separate files

Q: What if column names are different?
A: Rename them to: Unit, Chapter, Topic

Q: Can I process multiple times?
A: Yes! Upload and process any time

Q: CSV or Excel for Option 1?
A: Both available! Your choice
